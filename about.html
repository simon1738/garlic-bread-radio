Garlic Bread Radio started on 99.1 FM in fall of 2025, and it was moved to B-Side Internet Radio in fall of 2026. Listening to and discovering new music is one of my biggest hobbies, plus I'm studying computer science. So in 2026 I made this site as a fun way to bridge college radio and web development.


It's a not really an impressive tech stack (just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript), but the feature list was pretty simple so there was no reason to build anything more complicated than a static site.


Album cover images in the Setlists page are grabbed from Last.fm. Sometimes the API might return a cover from a song's single release, a compilation album cover, or no cover at all instead of the actual album art. So if anything looks weird in the setlists, it's not my fault...
